The thinking behind the lab

Small steps.
Clear decisions.
Useful tools.

M.E.N AI Code Lab is the trading name chosen for an independent sole-trader business focused on AI and automation development.

The approach begins with the people doing the work: what they need to know, where the repeated steps are, and what a useful result would look like.

AI is one part of that process. Clear instructions, appropriate permissions and a way for people to take over matter just as much.
